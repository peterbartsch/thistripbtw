Mission

Make it dead simple to plan, share, and keep a trip — without becoming the product.

Private by default

No ads, no trackers, no analytics, no data business. Your plans and memories are yours — kept private, never sold. Building a trip asks nothing of you at all; buying one takes the email Stripe already used for your receipt, and nothing else.

Simple enough for everyone

If grandma can't use it in thirty seconds, it's broken. One link, two taps, no sign-up to build, no app to install. The map is the whole interface.

You pay, so you're the customer

A few dollars once, and that's the entire model. No free tier that sells your attention, no upsell treadmill. Honest software with an honest price.

Together, then kept

A trip is a group effort and a shared memory. Everyone with the link can add to it while it happens — and when it's over, the whole thing stays put, right where you left it.

Readable and open

The client is being open-sourced so anyone can read exactly what it does. The best privacy promise is one you can check yourself.
