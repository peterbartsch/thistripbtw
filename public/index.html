Why we charge

You pay, so you're the customer — not the product. No free tier means no reason for us to harvest, profile, or sell anything. We don't run ads and we don't use analytics trackers. Building a trip asks nothing of you at all. Buying one lets you make an account if you want one — an email, a hashed password, and a list of which trips are yours. No name, no profile, no behaviour, nothing a data broker would pay for.

Plain-English inventory: we store the pins, notes, and photos your crew adds; which tier a bought trip is on; and, if you made an account, your email, a hashed password, and the list of trips that are yours. Nothing else. Payments are handled by Stripe (they know the buyer; we don't). Map imagery comes from OpenStreetMap-based tiles. Every trip has a delete-everything button that actually deletes everything.
