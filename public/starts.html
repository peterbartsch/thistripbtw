Starting points

Shapes, not itineraries. Open one and change every part of it.

None of these is a recommendation — they are the shapes trips come in, with placeholder places you replace. Opening one costs nothing and asks for nothing: no account, no card, no email. It is yours to edit the moment it loads, and only yours until you decide to keep it.

  • Two vehicles, meeting in the middle Some drive, someone flies in partway. Both paths on one map — the trip this was built for. 3 legs · open and change everything →
  • Classic road trip One vehicle, several stops, there and back. The shape most trips actually are. 3 legs · open and change everything →
  • The crew joins along the way You start alone; people arrive at different stops. Tag who is on which leg once it is yours. 3 legs · open and change everything →
  • Fly in, drive around Arrive by air, pick up a car, loop, fly home. Swap the airports for yours. 3 legs · open and change everything →
