If you were sent a trip link

A trip address is seven characters after the slash, like thistripbtw.us/xvsbe4r. They are deliberately hard to guess, which also makes them easy to mistype — check the link you were sent, and mind that there is no o, i, l, 0 or 1 in any of them.

If the address is right and you still land here, the trip has been deleted or its time ran out. Ask whoever sent it.
